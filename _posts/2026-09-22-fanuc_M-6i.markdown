Only an idiot would have one robot at home.

So here's my second - this time not torn apart, with a controller and _supposed_ to be in working condition, all for reasonable price (not including shipping). You know it's a reliable seller when they also have a concrete mixer in the background.  

I want to use it as is to send commands, test computer vision and programming projects. Moving heavy things is a pain, and I look forward to having a forklift or at least palet jack for the company in the future.
