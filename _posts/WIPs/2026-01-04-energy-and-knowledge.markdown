Energy used to have an initial cost and a running cost
Removing the running cost with solar/wind meant that prices would vary much more wildly
This creates both hedgers (battery installs) as well as opportunities for large consumers during trophs

This is comparable to knowledge, where it used to cost money to duplicate and disseminate knowledge. Internet allows for dissemination without costs, only left cost is upfront.
How this compares to energy? University: up front and ongoing cost, youtube video just up front cost

This means we could adapt our expectations of knowledge consumption and acquision to spread it over time, or spread it by availability, to better match both people and their learning methods as well as the economic realities of the world.

think eg learning a job upfront vs learning as you go, and what benefits vs disadvantages that could have.
