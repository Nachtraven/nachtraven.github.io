I've lived and worked in Flanders for a while, and wanted to bring my Dutch/Flemish to a level that would allow technical conversations. I realized that a lot of the language learning resources out there rarely had the type of intellectually engaging content needed to keep me watching to learn beyond B2. I also have had people ask me a few times what I watch in French.

An excellent way of enlarging your vocab is by listening in the target language with subtitles in both the language being spoken and your own language of choice, pushing yourself with some new vocabulary without overwhelming yourself. To have two subtitles at once on youtube, you can use [DualSub](https://www.dualsub.xyz/en/docs/intro.html)

Bellow are some nice Youtube channels for learning Dutch (with English and Dutch subtitles) as well as French (Often only with French subtitles). These are certainly not for beginners though, for those excellent resources exist such as [Easy Languages](https://www.easy-languages.org/)!

### Classifying content

S: Subtitles built in
+: Youtube CC subtitles in the target language
++: Youtube CC subtitles in 2 languages 
X: No creator made CCs, only youtube autogenerated

## Dutch/Flemish

1. [WeetJeDatOokWeer (++)](https://www.youtube.com/@weetjedatookweer) - Tom Scott like: General interesting fun facts videos
2. [De Ideale Wereld (S)](https://www.youtube.com/@DeIdealeWereld) - Real Flemish. Short social commentary videos. The subtitles often differ due to expressions or ways of speaking
3. [Universiteit van Vlaanderen (+)](https://www.youtube.com/@UniversiteitvanVlaanderen) - "Popular science" type videos with a variety of speakers

## French

1. [MrBidouille (+)](https://www.youtube.com/@monsieurbidouille) - Technical infrastructure and large scale engineering videos
2. [DirtyBiology (X)](https://www.youtube.com/@dirtybiology) - Long form technical and complex science videos
