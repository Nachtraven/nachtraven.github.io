We've witnessed an absolute explosion of the nerdy things that used to exist in magazines and hackerspaces into widespread adoption. From crypto, VR and 3D printing to drones and computer vision, as we all grew up these became industrial in scale and marketing. It used to be a decade ago that you needed to order 3D printer or drone parts individually and assemble them into your own package to have something even mildly affordable
