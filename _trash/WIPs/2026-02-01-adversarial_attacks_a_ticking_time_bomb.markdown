Every system can be compromised, such that it produces unexpected, undesireable or dangerous outputs. Your toaster can, by simply changing a single wire, be made to electrocute you; your camera can refuse to take photos of certain items, as is already the case with scanners and printers, and your AI assistance model can recommend inaccurate or downright dangerous acts, or even write backdoored code.

Adversarial attacks rely on the process of data compression, where information is condensed. This happens everywhere all the time: from calculators to google searches, large amounts of input information are condensed down into outputs with generally less but more refined contents.
